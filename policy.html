Return Policy

You must keep the original receipt in order for us to process any part return, core return or exchange.

If a part is purchased with a warranty, at part purchase, you can return the part within 30 days. Items must be the original part and must be properly marked. You must have your receipt for any return or exchange to receive a store credit. Labor to pull the replacement part or entry to the yard to pull the part is not included. Parts disassembled after purchase cannot be exchanged or returned.

Consequential and incidental damages are not recoverable under this Exchange Policy.
This Exchange Policy is only available to the original purchaser.

Nevada Pic A Part does not provide a refund nor an in store credit for admission fees. All parts are sold as-is with no returns, refunds or exchanges unless the warranty is purchased at the time you purchased the part. Nevada Pic A Part will not exchange or return any part after the 30 day warranty purchased expires. Parts disassembled void any warranty.

Nevada Pic a Part Core Deposit Refund Policy

Core Deposits will be refunded up to 30 days from the original purchase. The core item returned must match the actual part purchased. Core Deposits will be refunded with same payment that was used to purchase the part.

Nevada Pic A Part will not accept any core return after 30 days. You must have receipt for core refund.
