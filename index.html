What is an auto recycler

At Nevada Pic A Part, we care about the environment and do our part to recycle end of life vehicles, were not just auto wreckers. Our disposal process recycles all fluids from the vehicles (oil, antifreeze, power steering fluid, gas, etc.), all the cores that can be rebuilt are removed (radiators, starters, alternators, etc.) and shipped out for rebuilding. All of the ferrous and non-ferrous metals are recycled as well. We do our part. We recycle or reuse everything possible. Nevada Pic A Part is not your typical auto wreckers, we are your #1 source for auto parts in Las Vegas and Henderson.
